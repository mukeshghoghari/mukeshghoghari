Country Details

1. Australia has three main time zones: Australian Western Standard Time (AWST), Central Standard Time (ACST), and Eastern Standard Time (AEST).

2. Australia is ahead by 5 hours and 30 minutes than Indian time.

3. Some parts of Australia, like New South Wales and Victoria, observe daylight saving time, while others, like Queensland and Western Australia, do not.

4. During daylight saving time, AEST becomes AEDT (UTC+11) and ACST becomes ACDT (UTC+10:30).

5. Because of these variations, the time difference between the western and eastern parts of Australia can be up to three hours.
